What "best" means here. The game publishes no stat tables, so these picks rest on what wins on the public killboard, what the developer has said, and what things cost. Where the evidence is one player's post, the chip says so. Balance changes weekly; check the date on each chip.

Best change to a solo Wasp

Swap Fire Control for Autoloader II. Keep the Magazine Rack

Weapon cycle time is the wait. The Rack also lifts railgun and torpedo magazine and reload, so it stays. Calculated, with Autoloader II assumed at -10% cycle: railgun 1,980 to 2,340 damage over 90 s, flak 2,568 to 2,903, torpedo 2,074 to 2,765. The cost is Fire Control's +10% volley. Getting started

The short version of what a new pilot needs to know.

What you begin with

A corvette, a home station and the whole map. Official The Core is the safer starting region. Official

Where credits come from

  • The Agency pays for supply, hauling and combat work, and you can pick it up at any station. Official
  • One player's reading in October: the Agency is the only thing that creates credits. Trading only moves credits between players, and shooting random NPCs paid nothing. Player 10-07
  • Since then, NPC bounties in the Marches and the Deep were raised specifically as an income source, and more NPC loot can be sold directly. Patch 10-22, 10-26
  • NPCs carry bounties, but you need a bounty scanner fitted to collect them. That costs a module slot. Patch 08-03
  • The developer has said the Core is meant to be forgiving, with fast recovery from losses. Dev reply 09-01
  • The same thread rated manufacturing missions as the best value, because raw materials were in short supply on the market. Player 10-07 Manufacturing was reworked on 11 November, so re-check this. Patch 11-11
  • Agency payouts were raised on 11 November, with a bigger premium for work in the Marches and the Deep. Patch 11-11

Pace

  • Contract length scales with tier: about 5 minutes at T1, about 35 minutes at T5. Patch 11-09
  • You can switch mission tier inside the Agency screen. Patch 11-02
  • The galaxy map has a filter for Agency mission locations, and tapping a mission warps you to it. Patch 11-12 Player review

Habits worth having

  • Keep separate ships for combat, gathering and hauling. Official Cargo stays with a ship when you swap. Patch 11-05
  • Read the undock warning. It checks for missing drones, cap boosters and flares. Patch 11-05, 11-08
  • Favourite useful stations on the galaxy map. Patch 11-10
  • The flight path is the guided new-pilot track. It starts with combat. Patch 10-25, 10-31
  • Daily objectives can be re-rolled, and there is a daily login reward. Patch 10-25, 10-30
  • Uncovering a system also uncovers its neighbours, and autopilot works into unexplored systems. Patch 11-02, 11-10
  • Before leaving the Core, read Space and security. Loss rules change by region.

Everything the official site says about the setting. It is not much.

The Severance

Six hundred years ago humanity went through a gate it did not build, and then the route home went dark. Official The site calls the present-day space "the gate lanes beyond the Severance". Official Nobody in the setting knows who built the gate network. Official

The site shows a location stamp, First Gate Station K'LIA · FC 612, which fits a calendar counted from the crossing. Official What FC stands for is not stated.

Things that override the region

  • Corporation wars. A war can expose you to combat and full losses even in protected space. Official
  • Faction warfare. Dying in faction warfare can now also cost modules. Patch 11-09
  • Gray zones. A player describes a no-rules strip at the edge of each region's map where PvP is open. Whether you lose only gathered items or the whole ship there was asked and never answered. Player 09-23

Security level inside a region

  • Systems have a security level. T3 and higher bounties spawn in lower-security systems, and T3 and higher mining contracts ask for rare ore that is only found there. Patch 11-10
  • Navy ships now patrol systems owned through faction warfare. Patch 11-09
  • Core has no T2 ore sites and fewer rats than it used to. Patch 10-26, 10-30
  • Missions and bounties pay more in lower security. Patch 10-29

Reputation

  • Reputation loss scales with your standing and your highest mission tier. Patch 11-14
  • Killing an unflagged player costs roughly two missions' worth of reputation to earn back. Patch 11-14
  • Corp mates and fleet mates cannot attack each other. Duels exist for consensual fights. Patch 11-06
  • The IFF Transponder module was added on 10 November. Its effect is not described. Patch 11-10

Contraband and patrols

  • Patrol NPCs have a detection cone. Sitting in it does not always trigger a response. Player 10-07
  • If the navy keeps you detected long enough, it closes in, scrams your ship and searches it. If you are still there, the goods are confiscated. Player 10-07
  • Warp stabilizers let you warp out through the jam, but one pilot still had contraband seized instantly with them fitted. Player 10-07
  • A cargo scanner module exists. Patch 11-06
  • NPCs no longer drop contraband. Patch 10-25
  • NPC hails can offer contraband missions, and those are flagged as contraband. Hails were switched off in the Marches and the Deep on 23 October. Patch 10-23, 10-29

Kill records

There is a killboard. It counts Marches kills, leaves out locked hauling-mission cargo, and keeps PvE kill mails private to the pilots involved. Patch 11-01, 11-07, 11-11

In the Marches it does not count hull value. You can share killboard links in chat and save a fit straight from a kill mail. Patch 10-28

Deep heat

The Deep has a heat value. Mining and salvaging no longer raise it, and corp territory pushes it out. Patch 10-23 No source explains what heat does to you.

How fitting works

  • Four module families: weapons, defences, propulsion, utility. Each fit trades space, power use and performance. Official
  • Small hulls can dodge big guns. Heavy hulls bring durability and fitting room. Official
  • Capacitor is a real limit. One pilot could fit a Hardpoint Consolidator and a Salvaging Laser M and nothing more. Player 08-21
  • The fitting screen shows why a module does not fit, shows drone stats, and lets you compare a ship or module against its base version without proficiency. Patch 11-01, 11-04, 11-05
  • You can unload ammo from modules, and there is a reload-all button. Patch 11-01, 11-08
  • Ships have real hitboxes. Patch 10-28
  • Ships are tinted by their line type, and stored ships can be packaged. Patch 10-25
  • Escalation offers are capped at two tiers below your ship size. Patch 10-29
  • There is a fitting manager in the top bar of the fitting window. It saves fits and shares them. Patch 09-06
  • Smaller ships align for warp faster because they turn faster. The warp cast itself is a fixed length. One pilot timed corvette 4.5 s, frigate 5.25 s, destroyer 6 s from standstill to warp. Dev reply 10-06 Player 10-07
  • A shuttle costs 10 credits at any station. Patch 10-06
  • Job modules (mining lasers, salvagers, decoders) are limited to your hardpoint count. Industrial hulls get one extra. Patch 09-26, 10-15
  • Drone bandwidth differs by ship, and there are dedicated drone carriers. Patch 10-02
  • A fitting catalogue shows every item that fits a given slot. Patch 09-24
  • Building a ship needs materials one tier below the ship's tier. Patch 08-14
  • Generalist hulls have their own mild bonuses. Patch 10-01

Tiers

  • Gear runs T1 to T5. Player 08-21 Contracts also run T1 to T5. Patch 11-09
  • The account that posts the patch notes says the intended progression is hull size, small to heavy, and that T3 and above is min-max gear. A T2-fitted cruiser is the normal case, a T5-fitted one is not. Forum 08-21
  • T3 blueprints unlock when you reach T3, and you build the items yourself. Player 08-21
  • Gating materials reported in August: Salvaged Data Cores come only from T3 wrecks, and T4 ships and modules need Intact Drive Cores, a rare drop from T4 enemies. Player 08-21 Manufacturing has been reworked since. Patch 11-11

Proficiency

Mining hull yield multipliers: Quarry x1.25, Surveyor x1.50, Pathlight x1.70, Horizon x1.95. Dev reply 07-27

Each hull has a profession track. Your progress shows as an insignia in Fitting and on your nameplate, and equipped modules carry a proficiency badge. Patch 11-03, 11-04, 11-11 Pilots also develop specialisations. Official One named skill: directional analysis. Patch 11-03

How damage works

  • Two weapon classes. Kinetic weapons burn ammo and do a little extra damage to hull. Energy weapons burn capacitor and do a little extra to shields. Patch 08-20
  • Ammo tiers. Kinetic weapons can run dry, so higher-tier ammo is designed to lift their average DPS above the energy equivalent. Dev reply 09-14 Different ammo exists for each weapon type. Patch 08-18 There will be no range or tracking ammo; the developer turned that down for PvP reasons. Dev reply 09-14
  • Shield and hull multipliers. Each weapon lists one for each. Torpedoes: x0.9 shields, x1.1 hull. Player 11-10
  • Tracking. It measures how well a turret follows sideways movement. A small ship orbiting close is the hardest target, worst of all for big guns. A ship sitting still or flying straight at you is easy. Final damage depends on hull size, gun size, distance, sideways speed and evasion modules. Dev reply 08-17
  • Range. Weapon-family ranges are mostly the same across sizes, so a bigger hull does not automatically outrange a smaller one. Dev reply 08-17 Range was raised on 22 October. Patch 10-22
  • Rear arc. Hitting a target from behind does bonus damage; the arc is drawn on your current target. Patch 08-11
  • Cast weapons. Some weapons, railguns for one, have a cast bar. Casting slows you but does not stop you. Patch 08-22, 09-06
  • Speed-tanking. Fast targets take reduced torpedo damage; one pilot logged 25% hits. Player 10-29 Dev reply 10-30
  • Hitboxes and area attacks. Shots must connect with the hull shape. Area attacks respect PvP flags. Patch 10-25, 10-28
  • One target at a time. There is no multi-lock, and none is planned. Dev reply 09-05
  • Power starvation. A weapon with no capacitor waits and resumes; its button turns red. Patch 07-29
  • Global cooldown. 0.5 seconds between activations. Patch 07-28
  • Locking. Locks are instant and reach much further than weapons. Lock time is not planned. Dev reply 08-27
  • Ammo drops. Enemy ammo drops favour the weapons you have fitted. Patch 10-01
  • Faction gear. Elite pirates in combat sites can drop blueprints for faction modules, which need fewer special materials and have a twist on the standard stats. Random stat rolls were ruled out. Patch 09-02, 09-03

Size classes and hardpoints

  • Weapons and modules come in four sizes: Small, Medium, Large, Heavy. Patch 08-22

Core mechanics

  • You steer the ship, dodge telegraphed attacks and time active modules. Official
  • Range, positioning and tracking decide how well weapons hit. Official
  • Missiles fly to the target and hit on impact. They used to hit instantly. Patch 11-13

Where the highest payouts are

The developer identifies escalations as the highest-paying activity. The November 11 update raised Agency payouts and narrowed the gap. Combat missions can offer a chain. Legs keep the same tier but can move into lower security, with larger rewards in the Marches and especially in the Deep and wormholes. Using the cheap plates for ammo

The captured recipes also put a value on bringing plates home. With one Hull Fragment at 54 CR yielding 1.20 Reclaimed Alloy, three 10-CR plates and the 257-CR industry fee put a 1,500-belt batch at 341 CR. Two plates and the 86-CR fee put 500 canisters at 160 CR. These estimates exclude the trip and any refining or market fees. Against the earlier local-ore estimates of 395 and 203 CR, the trip saves just 54 or 43 CR per batch. A contract delivery or several batches makes a dedicated trip easier to justify. Mining for total ore or for a chosen mineral

The developer posted the actual module descriptions. Strip cycles faster, has higher overall yield and takes a broad blend. Core cycles slower, has lower total yield and concentrates on the primary mineral. In one player’s September test, a whole rock yielded 14 main and 4 secondary with Strip versus 14 main and 2 secondary with Core. That example shows why a purer load can still contain the same number of target units. For contracts compare target units delivered per minute and hold capacity. Mining drones automatically move to another asteroid, while lasers need another target. Mining laser descriptions · Player mining test · Drones and losses

Echo hacking and exploration income

The developer’s field-guide screenshot shows three puzzles. In Trace, match the displayed node sequence. In Relay, rotate nodes until the circuit connects. In Cipher, use the changing symbol rule to pick a key. Failed hacks can damage the ship or summon response ships. November added tutorials for the echo-site minigames and a rare blueprint drop from echoes. Very deep wormhole sites require a stronger scanner. Wormholes have full ship and cargo loss. Public sources give no numeric blueprint drop rate. Echo field guide · Tiers · Blueprints · Pay · Drones and losses

Progression and fittings

The developer described T2 equipment on a cruiser as ordinary progression and T3+ equipment as specialization. An early player cleared T3 sites in an autocannon Bulwark, then returned in a Quarry for salvage. That historical setup illustrates splitting jobs between hulls. Later patches changed salvage and manufacturing, so the old rare-drop experience is only a field report. Drone command range and leash range are separate. Once assigned, the drone can continue out to twice command range. Fitting progression · Blueprints

What changed in bounty missions

The November 14 rework shortened travel, reduced the number of targets and made them tougher. Targets no longer flee via the old event. Bribes occur less often and the Hunter event scales. T3+ bounties are placed in lower security. The mission-location map filter helps check the route before taking the job. Bounty rework · Tier and scanner changes · Payout fix

Exploration

Scanning and hacking

  • Hidden sites are scanned down and their caches hacked. Official
  • A Signal Scanner is needed for T3 sites. Player 08-21
  • Higher-tier ciphers were made a little easier on 12 November. Patch 11-12
  • The cipher hacking minigame was also eased on 25 October. Patch 10-25

Echo sites

  • Echo sites have minigames, with in-game tutorials since 2 November. Patch 11-02
  • The waveform minigame: three waveforms sit above a grey reference wave. Line each one up with the grey wave and lock it. Use the invert button on the left when a wave starts upside down. Lock order does not matter. Player 09-18
  • Enemies warp in on you while you work, and blueprints drop rarely. Patch 11-01
  • Double-click to hack an echo. Patch 10-29
  • Echo sites also appear inside wormholes. Patch 10-28

  • Wormholes carry modifiers. Patch 11-09

Corps, fleets and faction warfare

Group play, and the risks that come with it.

Fleets

  • Fleets no longer disband when a member goes offline, and you can see fleet members' coordinates on grid. Patch 11-08
  • Mining and salvaging contracts can be shared with the fleet, up to a fleet limit. Patch 11-07, 11-08
  • Fleet mates cannot shoot each other. Patch 11-06

Corporations

  • Corporations work toward shared upgrades. Official Known upgrades: corp storage tabs, and reduced manufacturing heat at a corp station. Patch 11-11
  • Corps own structures, outposts and stations, and set a headquarters. Move the HQ only after the station's hangar is unlocked. Patch 11-11
  • Leaving a corp starts a 48-hour lockout. Patch 11-03
  • The application screen warns you if the corp is at war. Patch 11-03
  • You can apply to a corp once every 48 hours. Patch 10-28
  • Corps have custom ranks and permissions, their own logo, and a recruitment chat channel. Patch 10-25, 10-28
  • Corp territory and structures first arrived on 20 October. Structure prices scale with size. Patch 10-20, 10-25
  • A corp directory ranks corporations, and new ones get a visibility boost. Patch 11-14
  • Corps have a member cap, kept low on purpose while the population is small. Dev reply 10-30
  • Corps can build sentries. A player reports they did not stop raids on a Deep home system. Player 11-14

Corporation wars and structures

  • A war can bring full losses in otherwise protected regions. Official
  • Declaring war costs a fee and uses a limited number of war slots. A treaty can end one. Patch 10-30
  • Pilots from corps you are at war with show red in local. Patch 10-29
  • Structures have a damage cap, so they take longer to kill. Patch 11-06
  • A corp station or outpost can only be attacked after every other corp structure in that system is destroyed. Patch 11-06

Faction warfare

  • Enlist with a faction to contest systems and capture control sites in a running territorial campaign. Official
  • Capturing a control point also pays INS. Patch 11-10 The notes do not say what INS is.
  • NPCs spawn on a control point after it is captured. Patch 11-09
  • Points are tracked separately for each faction and survive delisting and reset. Patch 11-11
  • You can delist from a faction after 48 hours. Patch 10-26
  • Control point status shows on the galaxy map. Patch 10-26
  • Held control points pay passively at 50%. Patch 10-07
  • Points are spent in the Faction Warfare Exchange. Patch 08-27
  • Control points have ship size limits and decisive periods. Patch 10-01
  • Dying can cost modules, shuttles are barred, and the navy patrols faction-owned systems. Patch 11-08, 11-09
